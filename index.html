Welcome to the pond.

Tap the water to send out ripples. The koi scatter, then drift back.

Press and hold, or double-tap, to scatter food.

Drag across the water to leave a wake. Move slowly, and a curious koi may follow.

Now and then it rains. The buttons in the corner turn rain and sound on or off.
